Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Platewell has no accounts and no sign-up. Everything you enter stays on your own device.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Every diet plan and every meal is built into the app. A connection is only used to load ads.
How do I change my calorie target?
It's worked out from your age, height, weight, activity level and goal. Redo the quiz from Settings → Redo quiz to change any of those. Your current plan keeps running. Logging a new weight also updates the target automatically.
I don't like one of the meals. Can I change it?
Yes — open the meal and choose Swap. You'll only be offered meals that fit the same plan and the same calorie budget, so swapping never breaks the day.
How do I avoid an ingredient?
Tell Platewell during the quiz what you never eat, and it plans around it. To change your exclusions later, redo the quiz.
How do I remove the ads?
Settings → Go Premium. It's a one-time purchase that removes the banner, the interstitial and the app-open ad permanently. Nothing else is locked — all 14 diet plans and every meal are free for everyone either way.
I bought the ad removal but the ads are back.
Tap Restore purchase, on the Go Premium screen or in Settings. This usually happens after reinstalling, or when the device is signed in to a different store account than the one used for the purchase.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, use Settings → Ad privacy choices. The option only appears where those rules apply. Elsewhere, ad personalisation is controlled by your device settings.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Reset all data erases your profile, plan, progress and weight history immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app removes everything too. There's no server-side copy, because there's no server.
Is Platewell medical advice?
No. It offers general wellness content — ready-made meal ideas and portion suggestions. Calorie figures are estimates. Please talk to your doctor before starting any diet, especially if you're pregnant, under 18, have a medical condition, or take medication.

Privacy
